# Hi, I'm Hedellen Pereira 👋

**Staff Frontend Engineer** building AI-native systems and micro-frontend platforms at scale.

I started my career over a decade ago writing frontend at a small agency, and I've spent every year since chasing the same question: how do you build software that stays reliable when thousands, then millions, of people depend on it not to break?

That question took me from agency websites to government portals used by a million people a month, to payments infrastructure processing at national scale, and now to leading the AI-native transformation of frontend engineering on high-stakes investment platforms. Along the way I learned that the hardest problems are rarely about writing code faster. They're about designing systems, and the standards around them, that let whole teams do their best work without fear.

Today I build **agentic systems in production**, not demos. And I believe the next generation of engineers won't be the ones who write the most code, but the ones who design the systems that let AI and their teams move fast, safely.

---

## 🧠 What I'm building

I design and ship agentic systems that change how fast teams can build:

- **AI-driven code pipelines**: Turning designs into production frontend code using MCP tool surfaces and AI agents, collapsing the handoff between design and engineering.
- **Product-memory agents**: Giving AI tools the context of a senior developer who knows the codebase, its patterns, and its history, so generated code fits instead of fighting.
- **Automated incident remediation**: Systems that watch, diagnose, and fix production issues before anyone gets paged.

I care about one thing above all: shipping fast without shipping recklessly.
